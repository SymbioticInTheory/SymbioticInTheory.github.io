Further notes from Victor Lekeu’s lectures, working through the physical degrees of freedom of Maxwell and symmetric higher-spin fields. Develops generalised de Donder gauge, residual gauge transformations and transverse polarisations, then constructs the Fronsdal action using trace constraints and the Noether identity associated with gauge invariance.
