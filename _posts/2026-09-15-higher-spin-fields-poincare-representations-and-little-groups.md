Notes from Victor Lekeu’s introductory lectures on higher-spin fields. Introduces the motivation for fields of spin greater than two, light-cone conventions, the Poincaré group and algebra, and Wigner’s induced-representation method. The worked material classifies massive and massless little groups and develops tensor representations using symmetry, trace constraints, and Young diagrams.
