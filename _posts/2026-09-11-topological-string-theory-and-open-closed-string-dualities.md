This dissertation develops the mathematical and physical foundations of topological string theory, beginning with bundles, complex geometry, Calabi–Yau manifolds, mirror symmetry, string compactification, and conformal field theory. It then treats Chern–Simons theory, cohomological field theories, topological sigma models, the A- and B-models, and Gromov–Witten invariants, culminating in the open–closed duality between Chern–Simons open string field theory on the deformed conifold and the topological A-model on the resolved conifold.
