Worked lecture notes on Wilson loops in supersymmetric gauge theories, from parallel transport and the static interparticle potential to Maldacena–Wilson loops in N=4 super Yang–Mills theory. Covers BPS loop configurations, perturbative ladder diagrams, the circular loop and its localisation to a Gaussian matrix model, the large-N resolvent, and connections between cusp anomalous dimensions and quark–antiquark potentials.
