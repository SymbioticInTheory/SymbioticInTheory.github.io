Worked notes on first-order point-particle and worldline models, developing BRST quantisation for bosonic and supersymmetric systems with ghosts, antighosts, auxiliary fields, and constrained Hamiltonian structure. The notes then move through Brink–Schwarz and superspace constructions, twistor descriptions of massless particles and superparticles, and the first steps toward pure-spinor formulations via ambitwistor ideas.
