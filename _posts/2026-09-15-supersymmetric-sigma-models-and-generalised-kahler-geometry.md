Notes from Ulf Lindström’s lectures on geometry and supersymmetry in nonlinear sigma models. Covers two-dimensional sigma models with metric and B-field couplings, N=(2,2) superspace, chiral, twisted-chiral and semichiral superfields, and the emergence of Kähler and generalised Kähler geometry from supersymmetry. The geometric discussion includes complex structures, integrability, and torsion.
