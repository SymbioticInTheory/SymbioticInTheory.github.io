Worked notes on gravity as a low-energy effective field theory, beginning with the Einstein–Hilbert action, the derivative expansion, graviton quantisation, and gauge fixing. The later pages develop a worldline description of black-hole binaries, including scale separation, tidal couplings, potential and radiation modes, and the Einstein–Infeld–Hoffmann Lagrangian.
