This archive brings together my worked notes in theoretical and mathematical physics: handwritten derivations, worked exercises, and reading and lecture notes. The collection ranges from quantum mechanics, statistical physics, and quantum information to quantum field theory, general relativity, supersymmetry, supergravity, and string theory. Alongside these are notes on the geometry, topology, algebra, and representation theory that underpin the physics.

The material is primarily at master's and graduate level, with more specialised explorations of twistors, scattering amplitudes, holography, and effective field theory. The emphasis is on working through the calculations and mathematical structures behind these subjects, with related topics connected through shared tags. My master's dissertation, Topological String Theory and Open–Closed String Dualities, provides a longer, sustained account of one of these areas.

These notes are rough in presentation because they were never written for other people; they are where I work through the details of what I am studying. You will find that some notes may seem out of place. This is a consequence of the file structure on my tablet. At times, while working through a subject, I was unsure about details in something new I was reading. To understand it better, I would often start a separate set of notes on that particular topic, work through it until I reached a point of understanding, and then return to the main subject. One example is Constrained Systems and Gauge Symmetry in the Twistors folder. That file grew out of my efforts to understand first- and second-class constraints while reviewing different quantisation models for the point particle; the related notes are collected in Point-Particle Models, BRST Quantisation and Pure-Spinor Ambitwistors.

Overall, I hope these notes convey my continued interest in the field and my ability to engage with advanced modern topics in depth.
