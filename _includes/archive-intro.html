This archive brings together my worked notes from the past three years in theoretical and mathematical physics: handwritten derivations, worked exercises, and readings of textbooks, lecture notes, and research papers, all of which I have studied in my free time. The material is primarily at graduate level, with more specialised explorations of twistors, scattering amplitudes, holography, and effective field theory. My master's dissertation, Aspects of Topological Quantum Fields, Strings and Dualities, provides a more coherent, put-together example of this work.

These notes are very, very rough (ugly) in presentation because they were never written for other people; they are where I work through the details of what I am studying. Within each category, many notes may seem out of place. At times, while working through a subject, I was unsure about details in something new I was reading. To understand it better, I would often start a separate set of notes about that particular concept I was unsure about, work through it until I reached a point of understanding, and then return to the main subject. One example of this is Constrained Systems and Gauge Symmetry in the Twistors folder. That file grew out of my efforts to understand first- and second-class constraints while reviewing different quantisation models for the point particle in my notes, Point-Particle Models, BRST Quantisation and Pure-Spinor Ambitwistors.

At the end of this page, you can also find many of my tutoring notes and session files, where I have continued to help students at the undergraduate and master's level.

Overall, I hope these notes convey my continued interest in the field and my ability to engage with advanced modern topics in depth.
